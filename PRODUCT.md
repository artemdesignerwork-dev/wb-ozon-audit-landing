# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS (one index.html + assets/, no build step). Fallback form posts to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co AJAX) which forwards to artemdesigner.work@gmail.com; needs one-time activation from the owner's inbox. Endpoint is `CONFIG.formEndpoint` in app.js.

## Users

Sellers on Wildberries and Ozon (small brands, solo sellers, managers of a few SKUs) who suspect their product card underperforms: low CTR, few orders, or a new card with no data yet. They evaluate on a phone or laptop between other work, are wary of agencies, calls and handing over cabinet access.

## Product Purpose

A free expert check of the card's main photo (cover) by a UX/Product designer, against 12 marketplace heuristics, with the three most important problems and what to fix first. The free audit is the entry into paid work: a full-card audit for 1 500 ₽, and then freelance orders for redesigning the cover and gallery (the designer writes the brief for the photo designer; does not shoot photos).

Success: the seller sends a card link in Telegram (primary) or via the fallback form.

## Positioning

A UX designer's heuristic audit, not a photo studio or an agency: 12 criteria built from WB and Ozon rules (2026), marketplace main-photo standards and Baymard research; scored report in Figma with a numeric grade, top-3 findings and a fix order. No calls, no cabinet access, answer within 24 hours.

## Operating Context

- Primary channel: Telegram @Artfreelancer (https://t.me/Artfreelancer).
- Fallback form: card link (must be wildberries.ru or ozon.ru), Telegram @username, optional "what worries you" (low CTR / few orders / new card / don't know), consent checkbox with a link to the data-processing policy.
- Deliverable: a report in Figma (cover with score /100, search-feed view, 12-heuristic table, top-3 problems, fix plan, next-step offer).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Free: main photo + 3 main problems. Paid 1 500 ₽: whole card, all 12 heuristics, Figma report, fix order.
- 12 heuristics in 4 groups: visibility in search, marketplace rules and penalties, information on the photo, trust.
- Scoring: each heuristic 0–2, total = sum × 100 / 24; a blocker scored 0 caps the total at 50.
- Seller status: самозанятый (НПД). ИНН and full name for the policy are not yet provided.

## Product Principles

1. Prove with a real report, never with claims or invented reviews.
2. Lowest possible friction: one link in Telegram, no calls, no access.
3. Say what to fix first; prioritisation is the value.
4. Free is genuinely useful on its own; paid is the natural next step, not a bait.
